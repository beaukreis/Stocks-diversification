## 4. Catalysts, Risks & Red Flags

**Growth drivers**

1. **Brand-led growth at home and abroad.**
   - DAHON is #1 on Tmall and JD.com.
   - Online direct sales are up 84%, overseas up 87%, and new dealer networks are opening in Thailand, Vietnam and the US/EU.
   - Hit products (K-Feather, Mariner D8) help lift the mix and gross margin.
2. **A deep-value balance sheet.**
   - Net cash is about half the market cap *(est.)*, and the dividend yield is about 9.5%.
   - Enterprise value is only about 3.3x 2026E profit.
   - A buyback or special dividend would close the gap quickly.
3. **Proof of durability.**
   - If H2-26 and 2027 show growth continuing (even at 15–20%) instead of the fad reversing, the stock should re-rate toward 10–12x earnings plus cash (≈HK$50–60).

**Risks and red flags**

1. **Fad and cycle risk.**
   - China's urban-cycling boom (2023–26) could fade, as the post-COVID bike boom did in the West.
   - That is my bust case (HK$20).
   - The folding-bike category is niche and competitive: Brompton, Tern (founded by a family rival), Java and Fnhon.
2. **Governance and earnings quality.**
   - The founder is 83, and rising outsourcing adds quality-control risk.
   - About 70% of sales go through distributors (channel-stuffing risk).
   - The cash figure is unverified.
   - The controlling shareholders' 12-month lock-up expired around September 2026, adding a potential share overhang.
3. **Liquidity and tariffs.**
   - The free float is small and illiquid: the stock went from a record oversubscription to −45%.
   - US and EU tariffs on Chinese bicycles affect overseas growth.
   - No meaningful sell-side coverage.
