## 4. Catalysts & Risks

**Catalysts**
1. **Pro-birth policy.** National childcare subsidies (from 2025), local birth incentives and insurance coverage of postpartum care.
2. **Asset-light expansion.** Managed centres, new cities and overseas sites (Singapore, US) raise returns on capital.
3. **H2 seasonality** and possible Stock Connect inclusion improving liquidity.

**Risks**
1. **Demographics.** China's births are ~9M a year and falling. Premiumisation has to offset volume decline.
2. **Accounting noise.** Fair-value swings make statutory profit volatile, and investors distrust "adjusted" metrics.
3. **Reputation and safety.** A single safety or hygiene incident in a care centre could damage the brand. The post-IPO lock-up expiries also added supply.
