## 4. Catalysts & Risks

**Catalysts**
1. **The global K-beauty wave.** Korean cosmetics exports keep setting records, and YesStyle is a leading cross-border destination in the US and Europe.
2. **H2 seasonality.** Black Friday and the holidays make H2 the larger half (H2-2025 revenue was ~US$257M).
3. **Rerating.** At ~9x earnings with 20–30% growth and a dividend, any institutional attention or index inclusion could lift the multiple.

**Risks**
1. **Brands going direct.** Korean brands are increasingly selling via Amazon, TikTok Shop, Olive Young Global and their own sites, which pressures YesStyle's take rate and selection advantage.
2. **Trade rules.** The end of the US de-minimis exemption and tariffs raise landed costs for parcels shipped from Hong Kong.
3. **Micro-cap governance and data.** It is founder-controlled with thin liquidity, and **I could not confirm the current share price or the share-count change.** Verify both before acting.
