## 4. Catalysts, Risks & Red Flags

**Growth drivers**

1. **A&D, drone and satcom demand.** A&D revenue more than quadrupled YoY to $2.2M, and catalog revenue was up 142%. The company is pushing into tactical radio (60+ RFICs, launched at IMS2026). Backlog doubled to $13.6M, which gives unusually good H2-26 visibility for a company this size.
2. **Operating leverage.** In Q2, revenue rose $2.6M YoY while opex fell 3%, so roughly 90% of the extra revenue reached operating income. Gross margin rose about 600 bps to 70.9%. Management is targeting full-year 2026 profitability.
3. **Possible Nasdaq uplisting.** Management says it is "actively evaluating" one. It could improve liquidity, open the stock to institutions and indexes, and narrow the OTC discount.

**Risks and red flags**

1. **Capital structure and dilution.**
   - The $22M Series A preferred ranks senior to common. It converts into 7.2M shares, about 67% of the current basic count.
   - There are 5.78M warrants, mostly struck at $2.50–3.05.
   - North Run controls two board seats. On a fully diluted basis it holds ~30% of the economics through the preferred, or ~40% including its warrants.
   - Below ~$55M of equity value the preferred keeps its preference rather than converting, so **common absorbs the downside first**.
   - An uplisting often comes with a share raise.
2. **Concentration and lumpiness.**
   - One distributor handles 65% of billings.
   - The top 3 end customers are 36% of revenue; the top 5 are 50%.
   - Automotive fell 28% YoY in Q2.
   - Past design-win revenue has not repeated: a $1.7M wireless-infrastructure win in Q2-24 did not recur.
   - The A&D surge could include program-timing pull-ins.
3. **Scale, competition and thin buffers.**
   - Revenue is ~$30M against ADI, MACOM, Qorvo/Skyworks and Mini-Circuits.
   - Cash is $4.6M.
   - Debt costs 12%, and principal amortizes $1.5M a year from 2027.
   - The stock trades OTC with thin volume after a ~250% run.
   - I could not confirm whether the FY-2025 10-K still contains going-concern language. It should be checked.
