## 4. Catalysts & Risks

**Catalysts**
1. **HBM4 at Nvidia and AMD.** HBM4 is sold out for 2026, and Samsung is closing the gap with SK hynix.
2. **Record capital returns.** ₩90–110T in 2026 plus ₩167T of net cash narrows the "Korea discount".
3. **Q3 preliminary results in early October.** Commodity DRAM and NAND prices are still rising.

**Risks**
1. **Memory cycle peak.** Margins of 52% are not sustainable, capacity is being added across the industry, and a sharp downturn is possible (my bear case).
2. **Non-memory weakness.** Foundry has lost share to TSMC, and mobile swung to a loss in Q2.
3. **Governance and geopolitics.** Chaebol control, US–China export rules, and North Korea risk.
