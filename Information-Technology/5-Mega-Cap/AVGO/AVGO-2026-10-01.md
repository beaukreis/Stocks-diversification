## 4. Catalysts & Risks

**Catalysts**
1. **Custom accelerator ramps.** Google TPU and additional XPU customers drive guided FY27 AI revenue of ~$115B. Q4 results come in December.
2. **AI networking share.** Ethernet is taking share in scale-out and scale-up networking (Tomahawk 6/Jericho).
3. **VMware cash flow** funds deleveraging and dividend growth.

**Risks**
1. **Customer concentration.** A few hyperscalers make up most AI revenue, and any customer that brings chip design in-house (or moves to a rival) is a large loss.
2. **Margin dilution.** Rack-scale system sales carry lower gross margins than chips.
3. **Leverage and a valuation built on FY27.** A capex pause would hit both earnings and the multiple.
