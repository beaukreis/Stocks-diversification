## 4. Catalysts & Risks

**Catalysts**
1. **Vera Rubin ramp.** Revenue per gigawatt of data-centre capacity rises from about $25B (Blackwell) to about $40B. That supports FY28 growth of ~70%.
2. **Earnings revisions.** FY28 EPS consensus rose 22% in 30 days, with 42 upward revisions and none downward. Q3 results in November.
3. **Capital returns.** The $80B buyback and the higher dividend set a floor under the stock.

**Risks**
1. **Concentration in a few hyperscalers.** Hyperscalers are ~55% of Data Center revenue, and an AI capex pause would hit quickly.
2. **Custom chips and AMD.** Broadcom/Google TPU, Trainium and MI400 could take share and squeeze 75% gross margins over time.
3. **Policy and power.** China export controls, possible tariffs, and power and data-centre build limits.
