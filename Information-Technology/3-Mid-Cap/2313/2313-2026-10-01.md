## 4. Catalysts & Risks

**Catalysts**
1. **LEO growth.** Brokers expect satellite revenue of **NT$18.5–20B in 2026 (+22–30%)**, rising toward a third of revenue as the largest customer's launch cadence and ground-terminal shipments increase. Brokers see LEO driving ~half of 2027E EPS.
2. **AI ASIC and optical-module boards.** Data-centre revenue is growing fastest, and the Thai plant adds capacity.
3. **Seasonal Apple build (Q3–Q4).** In past years Q3 has been the strongest quarter, so September and Q3 revenue (early October) are the test of the consensus H2 ramp.

**Risks**
1. **Consensus risk.** The 2026E EPS of NT$8.04 needs H2 profit to more than double. A miss would hit a stock at 28x 2026E.
2. **Customer concentration.** Its largest LEO customer (reportedly SpaceX) and Apple each carry high bargaining power, and a LEO customer could dual-source or insource.
3. **Capex and FX.** NT$9–15B of capex in a year, a strong NT$ (exports are in US$) and Thai ramp costs could dilute margins.
