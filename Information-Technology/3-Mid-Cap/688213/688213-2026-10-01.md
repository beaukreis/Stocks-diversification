## 4. Catalysts & Risks

**Catalysts**
1. **High-end phone sensors.** 50MP+ main and telephoto sensors are winning designs at Chinese phone brands (displacing Sony and Samsung), lifting average selling prices.
2. **Automotive CIS** (+ADAS camera count) and **AI glasses and robotics** vision under its "3+AI" plan.
3. **Q3 results (late October)**, given Q2's +18% QoQ revenue acceleration.

**Risks**
1. **Smartphone volumes.** The 2026 surge in memory costs is squeezing phone bill-of-materials and builds, which threatens sensor volume in H2.
2. **Foundry and supply-chain risk.** It depends on foundry capacity (incl. XMC, HHGrace, TSMC), and US export controls could tighten.
3. **Competition.** OmniVision (Will Semi), GalaxyCore, Sony and Samsung all compete on price in mid-range sensors.
