## 4. Catalysts & Risks

**Catalysts**
1. **Q3 results (early November).** Guidance is EPS NT$8.03, a fourth record quarter. August revenue was already NT$198B (+98%).
2. **AI data-centre and ASIC distribution.** Data-centre revenue rose 69% QoQ in late 2025, and management expects hyperscaler and AI capex to drive demand into 2027.
3. **Rerating from ~6.6x forward earnings.** Consensus targets sit at NT$237.5, and the ~4% yield supports the price.

**Risks**
1. **Memory-price dependence.** A large share of revenue is DRAM/HBM/NAND, whose prices have surged. When memory prices fall, distributor revenue and margins fall with them (my bear case).
2. **Leverage and working capital.** Growth is funded with debt, and margins are only ~2%. A demand shock could leave the company with excess inventory and expensive borrowing.
3. **The unexplained derating.** The stock fell ~30% from May despite record results, with heavy short selling in June. **The sources I could reach do not explain why.** The market may be pricing in information I could not find (for example, a franchise change, a large holder selling, or peak-memory fears).
