## 4. Catalysts, Risks & Red Flags

**Growth drivers**

1. **1.6T ramp and AI cluster scale-out.**
   - Orders reportedly run into 2027, and Q2 profit rose 38% QoQ.
   - Each new GPU generation (Rubin) raises both the number and the speed of optical links.
2. **Scale leadership and an overseas footprint.**
   - It is the largest supplier, with Thai plants that reduce tariff risk.
   - It has early 3.2T and silicon-photonics/CPO programmes, and the cost leadership to hold share.
3. **Shareholder returns and a cash pile.**
   - About CN¥6.4B was returned in 2026, and net cash is about CN¥60B.
   - That gives room for more buybacks and dividends if the stock keeps falling (−43% from the peak).

**Risks and red flags**

1. **Cycle and valuation.**
   - AI capex digestion would hit volumes and prices quickly, as in my bear case (CN¥213).
   - The stock has already fallen 43% from its high on "peak growth" worries.
   - Consensus implies 2027 profit about 5x 2025's.
2. **Technology and customer concentration.**
   - CPO and LPO could reduce demand for pluggable modules.
   - A handful of hyperscalers and Nvidia-ecosystem buyers dominate demand.
   - US export, tariff or entity-list actions are a constant risk.
3. **Insider and supply overhang.**
   - Major shareholders have been selling.
   - New H-share supply arrived (the H-shares broke issue on day one).
   - The mainland market is crowded and leveraged: margin buying topped CN¥1B in a single day in late September.
