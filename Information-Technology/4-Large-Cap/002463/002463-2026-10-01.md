## 4. Catalysts & Risks

**Catalysts**
1. **The switch to 1.6T networking and next-generation AI racks** raises layer counts and value per board, and WUS is the switch-board leader.
2. **Thailand scaling up** after turning profitable in Q2 diversifies tariff risk and adds capacity.
3. **Q3 results (late October)** extending the +60–70% growth trend.

**Risks**
1. **Valuation.** At 45x TTM and ~13x book, any slowdown in AI capex or loss of share would de-rate the stock hard.
2. **Industry capacity.** Victory Giant (CN¥18B of capex), Zhen Ding, Unimicron and others are all adding high-end capacity, which threatens 40% gross margins by 2028–29.
3. **US–China trade and concentration.** A few US hyperscaler and networking customers make up most of revenue.
