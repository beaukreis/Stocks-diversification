## 4. Catalysts, Risks & Red Flags

**Growth drivers**

1. **A direct play on AI-server build-outs.**
   - Higher-generation platforms (GB200 to Rubin, PCIe 5.0 to 6.0) need more layers, better materials and more board area per server, so revenue per server rises.
   - About 90% of PCB revenue is already server-related, and the company reaches 8 of the top 10 server makers.
2. **New capacity arriving into the shortage.**
   - The Thailand plant (offshore and tariff-resilient) is already profitable and adds capacity from Q4-26.
   - Huangshi is scaling up.
   - Capex is about CN¥5B in 2026. At full output, management expects about CN¥2 of revenue for every CN¥1 invested.
3. **Execution and margins.**
   - PCB gross margin is up 3.1 percentage points, and earnings quality is clean (cash flow ≈ profit).
   - A Q3 print in late October, a "national champion" position, and broker estimates that keep rising could support the A-share premium.

**Risks and red flags**

1. **Valuation and the A/H gap.**
   - The A-shares trade at about 57x TTM and **79% above the H-shares**.
   - The company itself sold H-shares at about half the A-share price, which tells you what it thinks the stock is worth to international investors.
   - My base value (CN¥114) is close to the H-share price, not the A-share price.
2. **Dependence on the AI capex cycle.**
   - With about 90% of revenue from servers and the top 5 customers at 61%, any pause in hyperscaler or AI spending hits volumes and pricing quickly.
   - Many capable rivals are adding capacity at the same time (WUS, Victory Giant, Shennan, Taiwanese makers), so margins could peak in 2027–28.
3. **Dilution and spending at the peak.**
   - The H-share IPO, a CN¥3.6B convertible bond and an H-share issuance mandate all add shares.
   - Capex of more than CN¥12B is committed near the top of the cycle.
   - The H-share cornerstone lock-up ended on 21 Sep (20.7M shares, 45% of the H-shares). Large holdings of restricted A-shares (274M) will unlock later.
   - US–China tariffs and export-control policy add risk.
