## 4. Catalysts & Risks

**Catalysts**
1. **Price hikes flow through from November.** DRAM foundry wafers are up 45% and logic is up 10–15%, so Q4 and Q1-2027 gross margins should rise above 30%.
2. **3D AI Foundry.** Volume orders for 4-layer WoW, the Intel EMIB IPD ramp (more than 10k wafers a month planned for 2027) and Micron HBM work.
3. **Monthly revenue and Q3 results (October–November)**, plus a possible full-year dividend.

**Risks**
1. **The cycle.** PSMC lost money for 10 straight quarters in 2023–25. Its profit now rests on memory prices and mature-node pricing, which Chinese foundries (SMIC, Hua Hong, Nexchip) are expanding into.
2. **Leadership change.** The founder who drove the WoW and Micron deals has died, and the acting chairman promises a more conservative style. That may slow the AI-foundry push.
3. **Valuation runs ahead of fundamentals.** The stock has ~700,000 shareholders and frequent limit-up days, so it is driven by sentiment and could fall sharply on any miss.
