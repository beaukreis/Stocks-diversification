## 4. Catalysts & Risks

**Catalysts**
1. **Overseas orders and new AI customers** land from H2-2026 into 2027 (high-layer and HDI boards for the next GPU and ASIC platforms).
2. **Margins recover** as the new plants fill and the extra staff become productive. A Q3 gross margin back above 34% would reassure investors.
3. **Q3 results (late October)** after a ~20% post-results drop in the H-shares.

**Risks**
1. **Customer concentration.** A large share of revenue comes from one GPU ecosystem (Nvidia), and losing share on a platform transition would hurt sharply.
2. **CN¥18B of capex at the peak.** If AI-board pricing normalises or the industry overbuilds (WUS, Zhen Ding, Unimicron and others are all expanding), returns would collapse.
3. **Earnings quality and valuation.** Recurring profit grew only 12.5% in H1 on a stock at 51x TTM.
