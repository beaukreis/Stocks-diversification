## 4. Catalysts & Risks

**Catalysts**
1. DRAM contract prices are still rising, monthly revenue is strong, and Q3 results come in mid-October.
2. Progress on 1C/1D processes and custom AI DRAM could narrow the technology gap with the top three makers.
3. A large FY-26 dividend is likely in 2027 after record profits.

**Risks**
1. **The cycle turns.** A 79.5% gross margin is exceptional, and every past DRAM peak has been followed by losses (Nanya lost money in 2023–24).
2. **Competition.** It trails Samsung, SK hynix and Micron on technology, and CXMT is adding legacy DRAM capacity.
3. **The fab timing.** NT$347B committed at peak prices could become stranded capital in a downturn.
