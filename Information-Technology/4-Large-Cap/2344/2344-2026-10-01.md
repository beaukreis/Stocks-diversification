## 4. Catalysts, Risks & Red Flags

**Growth drivers**

1. **A tight market with long contracts.**
   - Big DRAM makers are moving capacity to HBM, which starves legacy and specialty DRAM.
   - Customers want long-term agreements to 2029–30, and the CEO expects 2027 to be tighter than 2026.
   - Prices roughly doubled QoQ in Q2 (blended ASP +100%).
   - Over the next few quarters, earnings visibility is unusually high for a memory company.
2. **Capacity growth that lands in the shortage.**
   - Kaohsiung goes from 15k to 24k wafers a month by year-end, at about 88% yield on 16nm.
   - Its bit output roughly doubles in 2027, at peak-cycle prices.
   - Module B (16/14/12nm) has customers pre-booking capacity.
3. **Custom memory, NOR leadership and the Infineon deal.**
   - Custom memory (CMS) is now 53% of revenue, tied to AI-edge designs that are harder to swap out.
   - Infineon's NOR/F-RAM business, revived under the Spansion brand, lifts NOR share to about 34% with heavy automotive exposure. That is less cyclical revenue.
   - A large FY-26 dividend is likely to be announced around February 2027.

**Risks and red flags**

1. **Cycle-peak risk.**
   - Memory stocks typically peak 2–3 quarters before earnings do.
   - In July 2026, Micron, SK Hynix and Sandisk fell about 30% in a "memory boom goes bust" scare.
   - Industry capacity responds to shortages: Module B, peers' legacy lines and especially **Chinese DRAM makers (CXMT)** in legacy nodes.
   - My bear case (a 2022–23 style turn) is worth NT$58 (−68%).
2. **Spending at the top.**
   - Module B capex runs from 2027 to 2029, and the US$1.12B Infineon purchase closes in H2-27. Both are cash commitments made at peak prices and multiples.
   - If the cycle turns in 2028, these become the classic cyclical over-investment trap.
   - Winbond's dividend record is thin, so less of this boom may be returned to shareholders.
3. **Concentration and geopolitics.**
   - Fabs are concentrated in Taiwan, which carries cross-strait risk.
   - Demand depends on AI capex and on how major DRAM makers allocate capacity.
   - Consensus forecasts are stale and widely dispersed (NT$140–360 targets), so expectations could reset either way.
   - The stock is up about 5.3x in 12 months, so positioning is crowded.
