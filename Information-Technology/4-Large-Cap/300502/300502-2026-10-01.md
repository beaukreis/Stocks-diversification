## 4. Catalysts, Risks & Red Flags

**Growth drivers**

1. **1.6T volume ramp.**
   - The company says 1.6T has entered "sustained volume release", with shipments accelerating.
   - Supply is secured through advance procurement and prepayments.
2. **Industry-leading margins.**
   - A ~48% gross margin and ~36% net margin come from product mix and LPO strength.
   - The Thailand plant supplies export customers.
3. **More investors and capital through the H-share listing,** and inclusion in AI-infrastructure portfolios worldwide.

**Risks and red flags**

1. **Working capital and earnings quality.**
   - Heavy pre-stocking and prepayments mean that if demand pauses, inventory write-downs would hit margins. Inventory valuation is already a two-year key audit matter.
   - The Q1-26 QoQ profit dip triggered a sharp sell-off in April.
2. **Customer concentration and the cycle.**
   - A few hyperscalers and Nvidia-ecosystem buyers dominate demand.
   - AI capex digestion, or a shift to CPO, would hit a smaller player harder than Innolight.
3. **Valuation and dilution.**
   - Even my bull case (CN¥403) is below the price.
   - H-share issuance will add shares, and the stock has very high retail and margin participation.
