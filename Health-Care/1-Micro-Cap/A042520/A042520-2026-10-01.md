## 4. Catalysts & Risks

**Catalysts**
1. **CellREDM ramp through Hugel.** Clinic count is up ~60% in four months, and China and overseas approvals would extend the runway.
2. **BOUNCE implant re-entry and MINT Lift exports** diversify revenue beyond tissue grafts.
3. **FY-Sep-2026 results (December)**: the first full profitable year.

**Risks**
1. **Skin-booster competition.** Rejuran (Pharma Research), Juvelook and dozens of PDRN/PN/ECM products compete, and it is a fashion-driven category.
2. **Data and volatility.** The stock fell ~65% from its February high despite better results, sources disagree on figures, and it is a retail-driven KOSDAQ micro-cap.
3. **Regulatory risk.** Tissue products are tightly regulated (MFDS, FDA), and implants carry safety-litigation risk.
