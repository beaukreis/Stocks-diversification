## 4. Catalysts, Risks & Red Flags

**Growth drivers**

1. **Demand is still rising quickly.**
   - Patients on therapy more than doubled YoY, to over 49,000 at 30 June and over 50,000 in July.
   - First-line commercial access requires no step edits, and payer contracts run from 1 January 2026.
   - The endocrinology sales-force expansion and the DTC campaign widen the prescriber base.
   - The EU rollout is in its first year.
2. **The compensated-cirrhosis (F4c) outcomes trial reads out in 2027.**
   - A positive result would extend the label to F4c patients, who are at the highest risk.
   - It would also strengthen Rezdiffra as the backbone of combination therapy.
   - Two-year open-label F4c data already show improvements in liver stiffness and portal-hypertension risk.
3. **Operating leverage and a likely profit in 2027.**
   - Management says SG&A "steadies" after Q2, and Q2 was cash-positive.
   - Each extra $100M of revenue adds roughly $85M of gross profit.
   - Big pharma's MASH acquisitions (Novo–Akero, Roche–89bio for $3.5B) make MDGL a plausible strategic target.

**Risks and red flags**

1. **Patent cliff and generic challenges.**
   - The composition patent expires in September 2026. NCE exclusivity ends in March 2029.
   - Generic Paragraph IV filings can start in March 2028, inside the 5-year window.
   - Crystal-form patents end in 2033. The 2045 dosing patent has not been tested in court.
   - IRA price negotiation could also apply to a small-molecule drug about 9 years after approval.
   - This is the single biggest valuation swing factor.
2. **Competition and pricing.**
   - Wegovy was approved for MASH in August 2025, and GLP-1s already reach most MASH patients.
   - FGF21 drugs are coming from Novo (efruxifermin) and Roche (pegozafermin, Phase 3 data in H1-27).
   - Combination regimens could make Rezdiffra an add-on rather than the backbone.
   - Gross-to-net is already in the high 30s %.
3. **Single product, spending heavily, and a stretched valuation.**
   - 100% of revenue comes from one drug.
   - SG&A runs at about $1.1B a year, and the company is still loss-making.
   - Cost of sales jumped 339% YoY (higher royalties plus an inventory write-down).
   - The event-driven F4c trial could fail.
   - At about 10x 2026 sales, the stock needs both blockbuster-scale sales and durable patents.
