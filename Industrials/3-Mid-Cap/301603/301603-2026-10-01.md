## 4. Catalysts & Risks

**Catalysts**
1. **Machines for AI liquid-cooling parts.** Demand for cold plates, manifolds and CDUs is growing quickly with GPU racks.
2. **Domestic substitution** of Japanese, Taiwanese and German machining centres, plus robotics and humanoid-parts machining.
3. **Q3 results (late October)**, with the order book up 54% at the March quarter-end.

**Risks**
1. **Cyclicality.** Machine-tool demand follows China's manufacturing capex and 3C (consumer electronics) cycles.
2. **Competition and price pressure.** Haitian Precision, Kede and many smaller players compete with it.
3. **Receivables.** Cash conversion lags profit, and customers are often small and medium-sized manufacturers.
