## 4. Catalysts & Risks

**Catalysts**
1. **The South-East Asia fab build-out.** Memory and HBM fabs (Singapore), TSMC, and the advanced-packaging plants moving to Singapore and Malaysia. New orders announced in Q4 could lift backlog visibility.
2. **TSMC Nanjing and mainland work** add a second leg.
3. **Q3 results (late October)**, given Q2's +207% profit growth.

**Risks**
1. **Margin reversion.** A ~19% net margin is far above the contractor norm. Project close-outs and claims may be flattering the result.
2. **Lumpiness and concentration.** A few fab owners make up most revenue, and FY-2025 revenue fell 9% between projects.
3. **Related-party structure.** The Taiwan parent decides which group entity books which projects, and the listed company's share of the CN¥70B backlog is not disclosed.
