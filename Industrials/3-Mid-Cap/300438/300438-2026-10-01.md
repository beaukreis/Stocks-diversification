## 4. Catalysts & Risks

**Catalysts**
1. **The global storage boom.** Grid-scale storage in China, the Middle East and Europe, plus AI data-centre backup, has the company at full utilisation.
2. **Overseas mix.** Exports carry a 24.7% gross margin against ~16% domestically.
3. **Q3 results (late October)**, the first quarter with new capacity contributing.

**Risks**
1. **Overcapacity and price wars.** In 2023–24 storage-cell prices halved and Great Power lost money.
2. **Lithium costs.** Lithium carbonate has roughly doubled from 2025 lows, and pass-through lags squeeze margins.
3. **Leverage and tariffs.** Expansion is debt-funded, and US tariffs and FEOC rules exclude Chinese cells from parts of the US market.
