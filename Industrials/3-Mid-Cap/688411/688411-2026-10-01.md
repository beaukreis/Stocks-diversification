## 4. Catalysts & Risks

**Catalysts**
1. **H2 delivery season.** Storage installations in China peak in Q4, which should turn the inventory into revenue and cash.
2. **Overseas expansion.** Its export margin is 29.5%, and it has a 1.6 GWh German project and 420+ MWh delivered in the US.
3. **Q3 results (late October)**, where the key test is operating cash flow.

**Risks**
1. **Low barriers.** Integrators compete with cell makers that integrate forward (CATL, BYD, EVE) and with inverter makers (Sungrow, Huawei).
2. **Working capital.** The inventory surge plus long receivable cycles on state-owned projects could turn into write-downs.
3. **Policy.** China's storage market depends on provincial capacity payments and market rules after the 2025 end of mandatory storage for renewables.
