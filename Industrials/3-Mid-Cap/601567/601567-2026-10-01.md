## 4. Catalysts, Risks & Red Flags

**Growth drivers**

1. **Overseas distribution is the real growth engine.**
   - The overseas backlog is CN¥2.80B (+28%).
   - Wins include the Enexis framework (its first high-end Western European utility), Finland, Swedish charging, and a **US$132M US data-centre transformer order** (Sep 2026).
   - Grid upgrades in Europe and the US, and data-centre power demand, are multi-year themes, and transformers are in global shortage.
2. **Domestic margins should bottom out.**
   - The 2025 profit collapse came from **delivering State Grid orders won at low tender prices**, plus higher raw-material costs.
   - As those orders run off and new tenders reprice, gross margin should recover from 24.8%.
   - Q2-26 profit (CN¥379M) was already more than double Q1's (CN¥167M). State Grid's investment budget is at a record level.
3. **Dividends and a re-rating.**
   - The minimum 45% payout policy and the HK listing broaden the investor base.
   - At about 17.7x 2026E and 13.9x 2027E, the stock is below the CN electrical-equipment average (~46x TTM).
   - Proof that margins have bottomed (a Q3 print around late October) could close part of the gap.

**Risks and red flags**

1. **The earnings decline may not be over.**
   - Recurring profit fell 63.5% in H1. Gross margin is down about 7 points in 2025 and still falling.
   - Domestic tenders are price-competitive, against Linyang, Haixing, Wasion and others.
   - Brokers keep cutting estimates: the 2026E consensus fell from CN¥1.65B to about CN¥1.22B.
   - 24% of H1 profit was non-recurring.
2. **The medical business is a drag.**
   - Rehabilitation hospitals face insurance payment reform, which already caused a CN¥356M goodwill impairment in 2025, and more could come.
   - The company says it has "no plan" to sell them, so the conglomerate discount stays.
3. **Governance, dilution and trade risk.**
   - The controller's AUX group is a related party, has taken large dividends, and has a history of acquisitions.
   - The HK H-share issue will dilute A-share holders.
   - FX swings hit finance costs. US tariffs and trade policy on Chinese electrical equipment are a risk to the US data-centre order.
   - The dividend currently exceeds earnings (TTM payout ~150%), so it will be cut.
