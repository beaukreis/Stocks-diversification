## 4. Catalysts & Risks

**Catalysts**
1. **Category expansion.** Whitening serum oil (launched May 2026) and the 小金珠 toner (#1 toner on Douyin) reduce dependence on facial oils.
2. **Double 11 (November) and the H2 peak**. Its 618 omni-channel GMV more than doubled.
3. **Stock Connect flows and dividends.** A rerating could follow from southbound buying and a high payout.

**Risks**
1. **Marketing intensity.** Selling and marketing take more than half of revenue, and Douyin traffic costs keep rising.
2. **Competition.** Proya, Chando, Mao Geping and international brands are all cutting prices in China's prestige skincare.
3. **Overhang.** The stock trades 28% below its IPO price; lock-up expiries and weak China consumer sentiment could weigh further.
