## 4. Catalysts & Risks

**Catalysts**
1. **US retail expansion** (Ulta, Costco, Target) on top of Amazon and TikTok Shop, plus **Europe** (+380%).
2. **Q3 results (early November) and Black Friday.** Consensus may still be too low after Q2 beat estimates by ~10%.
3. **New hero products.** PDRN and collagen lines, plus device upgrades (AGE-R Booster Pro).

**Risks**
1. **Fad risk.** K-beauty brands rise and fall on social-media virality. Medicube is now ~half US-dependent.
2. **US tariffs.** Korean goods face a 15% US tariff, which pressures margins or prices.
3. **Concentration in Amazon and TikTok channels**, plus growing competition from other K-beauty brands (Anua, Beauty of Joseon, COSRX).
