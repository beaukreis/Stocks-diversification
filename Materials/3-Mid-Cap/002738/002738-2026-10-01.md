## 4. Catalysts & Risks

**Catalysts**
1. **Lithium prices.** Storage-driven demand has pushed carbonate to ~CN¥170k/t, and each +CN¥10k/t adds ~CN¥0.4–0.5B of annual profit *(est.)*.
2. **Kitumba copper** starts in Q3-2026 / Q1-2027, adding a third earnings stream when copper prices are high.
3. **Q3 results (late October)**, with full lithium-price leverage.

**Risks**
1. **Lithium volatility.** Prices fell ~85% in 2023–24, and Q2-2026 already showed a spike-and-fade pattern.
2. **Jurisdiction risk.** Zimbabwe (Bikita: export bans and royalties), Zambia and Namibia.
3. **Execution.** Tsumeb has lost money, and Kitumba is a first copper build for the company.
