## 4. Catalysts & Risks

**Catalysts**
1. **Phase 2 lifts output to 13,000t+ in 2027**, about 45% more volume.
2. **Tungsten's strategic premium.** China's export controls, defence demand and Western supply-chain diversification favour non-Chinese supply, and Bakuta is one of the few large non-Chinese mines.
3. **Capital returns.** The interim dividend plus up to HK$200M of buybacks.

**Risks**
1. **Tungsten price.** The H1-2026 price was exceptional. A reversal to ~CN¥300k/t would halve profit (my bear case).
2. **Single asset and jurisdiction.** Kazakhstan tax, royalty and permit risk, and a sales route through China.
3. **Governance.** The chairman resigned on 16 Sep, and the stock is down ~42% from its high and was heavily shorted earlier in the year.
