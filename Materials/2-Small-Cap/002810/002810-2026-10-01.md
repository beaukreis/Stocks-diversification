## 4. Catalysts, Risks & Red Flags

**Growth drivers**

1. **Plant-capsule momentum.**
   - Volumes and average prices are both up.
   - Being integrated back into its own HPMC raw material gives a cost advantage.
   - Demand is structural: vegan/halal supplements, pharma, and the move away from gelatin.
2. **Operating leverage from utilisation.**
   - Cellulose-ether utilisation (~76%) and capsule capacity still have room to rise, and unit costs are falling.
   - H1 net margin of 17.4% was more than double FY-25's.
3. **US capsule plant.**
   - Local supply for US supplement and pharma customers avoids tariffs.
   - It could lift both the business mix and the valuation (Shenwan sees a long growth runway).

**Risks and red flags**

1. **Cyclical, commoditising cellulose ethers.**
   - Construction-grade HPMC depends on China's weak property market and is in an oversupplied price war.
   - Export markets face trade-defence risk.
2. **Capex and leverage.**
   - Building the US plant plus domestic expansion means a convertible bond, rising net debt and weaker free cash flow.
   - Execution risk on a first overseas plant.
3. **Valuation already reflects the rebound.**
   - It is 31x TTM, and my base value is 13% below the price.
   - Earnings were volatile in 2024–25 (2025 −36%).
