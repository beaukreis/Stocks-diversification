## 4. Catalysts & Risks

**Catalysts:** (1) Syama recovery (explosives supply, fleet, A21 high-grade pit); (2) Doropo construction milestones toward ~170 koz a year; (3) gold above US$4,000.
**Risks:** (1) **Mali sovereign risk** (junta, mining-code changes, asset seizures); (2) execution, with repeated guidance cuts (Oct 2025 and Sep 2026); (3) cost inflation and royalties that rise with the gold price.
