## 4. Catalysts & Risks

**Catalysts**
1. **Policy support.** The current government favours local currency (consumption coupons, larger national subsidies for local-currency discounts). The 2027 budget is decided in Q4.
2. **Shareholder returns.** A ~5% dividend yield plus buybacks at ~6x earnings, and Korea's "Value-up" programme encourages a rerating.
3. **New services** built on the platform: B2B prepaid, public payments and overseas pilots.

**Risks**
1. **Policy and budget dependence.** Local-currency subsidies were cut sharply in 2023–24, and earnings follow government budgets.
2. **Contract renewals.** Municipal contracts are tendered and can be lost to competitors (KT, Chak and others).
3. **Lower rates** reduce float income, and a large share of H2-2025 profit was one-off coupon volume.
