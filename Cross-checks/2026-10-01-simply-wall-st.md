# Simply Wall St Cross-Check, 1 Oct 2026

All 42 reports were compared with Simply Wall St (SWS): their analyst fair values, DCF estimates, snowflake checks and flagged risks. Each report now ends with a **Simply Wall St cross-check** section giving the verdict and reasoning.

**How this was done.** The owner's logged-in SWS account (and its AI assistant) could not be reached from the cloud session that ran this review. The comparison uses SWS's **public** stock pages and articles found through search. SWS snapshots carry different dates, so where an SWS price looked older than mine, I kept the more recent dated price. Where SWS's data was clearly newer (it matched post-H1-2026 earnings), I updated to it.

## What the cross-check caught

1. **Wrong or stale prices in four reports.** These were the most important fixes.
   - **TOYO**: my $14.96 was a quote for a different ticker (TYO). The real price is ~$4.24, so the stock moves from "overvalued" to "deeply discounted, with policy risk". It also moves to 1-Micro-Cap.
   - **K92**: I used a July C$20.60 quote. The price was ~C$30.5 in late September, so the upside to my base falls from +99% to +35%.
   - **YesAsia**: ~HK$2.96 (not HK$3.65).
   - **Austral Resources**: ~A$0.065 (not A$0.084), with ~2.5B shares (not 2.0B).
2. **An earnings-quality flag I had missed (YesAsia).** SWS flags a high share of non-cash earnings and insider selling. Profit is not turning into cash (TTM profit ~US$27M vs US$16M of cash), so I had wrongly ticked "high-quality earnings".
3. **Consensus well below my price on Samsung.** The SWS analyst fair value (~₩267k) and the most-followed narrative (~₩209k) both sit below the price. That supports my DCF and argues against a high near-term score.
4. **Peer-relative valuation checks I had scored too harshly.** Victory Giant, WUS and HyperStrong trade below SWS's peer and industry P/Es. These are the same checks SWS's snowflake uses, so the valuation pillars were raised. My DCFs, however, still say these stocks are expensive.
